Who Are We?

Student Interest Group for Machine Learning (SiGMaL) is a community-driven initiative centred around Python and its diverse applications, particularly in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and computational problem-solving.​ Our aim is to enrich students' academic and technical journeys by fostering a thriving virtual community driven by online volunteering, engagement, creativity and collaborative learning.

We strive to bring together Python enthusiasts, beginners, tinkerers, researchers and industry practitioners to learn, share knowledge and explore the possibilities of the Python ecosystem.​ Everything we run is free, volunteer-led and open to everyone.
Moving forward, we ​p​lan to grow SiGMaL into a larger, more inclusive​ and vibrant community that encourages collaboration, knowledge-sharing, innovation​ and meaningful connections among our members.

FAQ

Do I need prior ML experience?

No. We run beginner-friendly sessions alongside advanced ones, and each event is labelled so you know what to expect.

Is it free?

As of now, every event has been free for attendees. We may need partners or sponsors for future events to keep the initiative free for students.

How can I give a talk or propose an event?

Check the Speak section. First-time speakers or organizers are very welcome and we'll help you shape the idea.

Where can I find past talks and materials?

Slides, notebooks and recordings from past sessions are on our GitHub and YouTube channel, linked in the footer.

How to get in touch with the team?

Zulip, Whatsapp or send us an email. Please understand that everyone in the team is contributing voluntarily, the passion keeps the initiative running. Please refrain from calling and reaching out through multiple channels.. give us time to reply back. We do check all emails and messages.

Code of Conduct

SiGMaL is a harassment-free space for everyone. Be respectful, be kind, credit others' work and ask before you assume. If something feels off, tell any core or write to sigmalcommunity@googlegroups.com and we'll act on it. Read More.

Privacy Policy

All registration data remains safely with the team and is never shared externally. Please refer to Zulip's policies to understand how they collect data when you interact in the forum.

Terms

As this is a voluntary, non-commercial interest group, all members participate entirely of their own free will. In the event of any disagreement, dispute or difference of opinion, the decision of the steering team and core members shall be considered final. By joining the group, members voluntarily acknowledge and agree to abide by its CoC, guidelines and decisions.

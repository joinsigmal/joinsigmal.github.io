Code of Conduct & Reporting

SiGMaL is a harassment-free and inclusive online space for everyone. We follow the Python Software Foundation's Code of Conduct to build an open and welcoming community. Be respectful, be kind, credit others' work and ask before you assume.

All members, including but not limited to participants, speakers, organizers and other community members, are expected to adhere to our Code of Conduct during online discussions and at all community events.

Unacceptable behaviour includes discriminatory or exclusionary comments, harassment, bullying, intimidation, threats, insults, offensive jokes, unwanted sexual attention, sharing inappropriate images, disrupting discussions or community activities, encouraging violence, sharing private conversations or recordings without consent and continuing unwanted interactions after being asked to stop. Such behaviour will not be tolerated.

If something feels off, please do not hesitate to report it by emailing us at sigmalcommunity@googlegroups.com. We take every report seriously and are committed to addressing concerns appropriately.

The identities of both the person submitting a report and the individual being reported will be kept strictly confidential and will not be publicly disclosed. To promote transparency and accountability, we may maintain a record of reported incidents and the actions taken, without revealing anyone's identity. This will help reinforce community standards, raise awareness and discourage similar incidents in the future. Our goal is to foster a respectful, inclusive, and safe environment for everyone.
